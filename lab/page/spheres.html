SPU Material Spheres

In SPU a material is two dials. Finish is what the surface is like. Body is what happens to light that gets past the surface. Drag them, pick a preset below, and move the light across the stage.

The whole map

Finish down the side, body across the top. Every ball is the same clay color under the same light, so only the dials differ. Picking one keeps your color.

Named materials
